Strengths of Acids and Bases & Ionization Constants

Core concept

🌱 A strong acid or bases will always break into its component ions when it comes into contact with water.

🌱 In other words, all molecules of a strong acid or base will produce its ions.

🌱 Interestingly, the concentration of the strong acid/base must be equal to the concentration of its products.
Just because it prodices few, doesn't mean it's weak!

🩸 On the other hand, a weak acid or base doesn't always ionize when it comes into contact with water.

🩸 Only a fraction of its molecules do at a time!

🩸 The ratio between the concentration of ions and of the weak base is called the ionization constant (Kb/Ka)!

🩸 We measure these concentrations when they aren't chanigng anymore, or are in equilibrium! So we actually use the same formula for chemical equilibriums! And ICE tables!
